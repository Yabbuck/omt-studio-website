TWO SIDES OF THE SIGNAL

One app. Two workflows.

OMT Studio is being developed around two focused areas for Android.

01 IN DEVELOPMENT

MONITOR

See what's on the network.

Discover OMT sources on the local network and receive them on an Android device for monitoring.

  • Source discovery
  • Video / audio receiving
  • Fullscreen monitoring
  • Diagnostics
02 PLANNED AREA

CAMERA

Turn Android into a source.

Use the device camera and microphone as an OMT source for compatible receivers on the network.

  • Android camera input
  • Microphone input
  • OMT source output
  • Receiver workflows
