MONITOR / CAMERA

Two ways to work with OMT.

Use Monitor when you need to receive and inspect an OMT source. Use Camera when the Android device itself should join the network as a source.

ANDROID / OMT ROUTING BIDIRECTIONAL
SIGNAL PATH
DISCOVER · RECEIVE · SEND ↗

01 / MONITOR

Monitor the signal.

Find OMT sources on the local network and keep a practical view on video, audio and receive status.

  • Local OMT source discovery
  • Preview / Full receive modes
  • Receive quality control
  • Audio monitoring
  • Fullscreen viewing
  • Live bitrate & diagnostics
  • Portrait and landscape sources

02 / CAMERA

Become the source.

The camera you already carry can become part of the production network — with OMT Studio handling capture, audio and sender control.

  • Direct Camera2 capture
  • Device-aware resolution & FPS
  • 4K / UHD capture
  • Focus, exposure, shutter, ISO & white balance
  • OMT sender quality control
  • Camera + microphone audio
  • OMT tally
  • Thermal Guard
