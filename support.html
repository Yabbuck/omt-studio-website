Bug reports

Please include enough context to reproduce the issue:

  • OMT Studio version and Android version
  • Device model
  • Monitor or Camera workflow affected
  • Source / receiver involved
  • Steps to reproduce
  • What you expected and what happened

Remove personal information and sensitive network details from screenshots or logs.

What are you trying to achieve, what other tools are in the signal chain, and where does the current workflow slow you down?
